The Chinese Tea Ceremony

The Chinese Tea Ceremony ( 敬茶, jìng chá ) is one of the most significant and beautiful traditions in a Chinese wedding. It serves as the formal introduction of the bride and groom to each other's families.

Watch the Demo

Via Google Drive

A Sign of Respect

By serving tea to their parents and elders, Mikhail and Ain express their deep gratitude and respect for the love, support, and upbringing they have received.

The Blessings

After drinking the tea, the elders will offer their wisdom, blessings for a happy marriage, and present gifts—usually jewelry or an Ang Pow (Red Envelope).

Merging Families

This ceremony officially welcomes the bride and groom into their new respective families. We are thrilled to share this cultural merging with all of you!
